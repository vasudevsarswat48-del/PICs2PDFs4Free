    About converting PNG files to PDF

    PNG is popular for screenshots, graphics, logos and scanned documents because it keeps text and sharp edges crisp. Turning PNG files into a PDF is ideal when you want to share a set of screenshots or hand in a document as a single file.

    PNG images can contain transparent areas. When converting, transparent pixels are filled with white so your PDF pages look clean and print correctly.
