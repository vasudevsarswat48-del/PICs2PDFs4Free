Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Pics2PDFs4Free uses cookies and similar technologies such as local storage.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help sites work, remember preferences and measure or personalise advertising.

Cookies and storage we use

  • Essential / preference storage: We save your theme choice and your cookie-banner choice in your browser's local storage. These are not shared with third parties.
  • Advertising cookies: Google AdSense and its partners may set cookies (such as DoubleClick) to show relevant ads, limit how often you see an ad and measure ad performance.
  • Analytics: If enabled, analytics providers may set cookies to measure aggregated traffic.

Managing cookies

  • Use the banner on our site to accept or decline optional cookies.
  • Control personalised ads at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info/choices.
  • Delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how parts of the site work.

More information

See how Google uses cookies in advertising at policies.google.com/technologies/cookies, and read our Privacy Policy.
