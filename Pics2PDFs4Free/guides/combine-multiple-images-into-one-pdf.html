How to Combine Multiple Images into One PDF (Free, Step by Step)

Updated: October 30, 2026

Sending ten separate photos of a document is messy. A single PDF is neater, keeps the pages in order and opens on any device. Here is how to merge pictures into one PDF in a minute.

Step-by-step

  1. Open the Pics2PDFs4Free image to PDF converter.
  2. Click the upload box and select all the images you want. On a phone you can select several photos from the gallery at once.
  3. Drag the thumbnails into the right order. Use the rotate button for pictures that came out sideways.
  4. Pick A4 or Letter for printing, or "Same as image" to keep the original proportions.
  5. Click Convert to PDF. The merged file downloads automatically.

Tips for a better result

  • Name your files 01, 02, 03… before adding them, so they load in order.
  • Use Medium quality for email attachments; use High for printing.
  • Leave a small margin if the PDF will be printed, so edges are not cut off by the printer.
  • Photograph documents in good light and straight-on for the cleanest pages.

Why not use an online service that uploads files?

Many converters upload your pictures to a remote server. For personal documents that is an unnecessary risk. Pics2PDFs4Free does everything in your browser, so your files never leave your device.
