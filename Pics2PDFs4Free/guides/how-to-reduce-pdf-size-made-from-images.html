How to Make a Smaller PDF When Converting Photos

Updated: October 30, 2026

A PDF built from phone photos can easily reach 30 MB or more, which many email services and upload forms reject. The good news: you can shrink it a lot with a few simple choices.

1. Lower the quality setting

Photos from modern phones are 12 megapixels or more — far above what a screen or an A4 page needs. Choosing the Medium or Low quality option in our converter reduces image resolution and compression, often cutting the file size by 70% or more.

2. Crop out empty space

Crop your photos before converting so the document fills the frame. Less unnecessary background means smaller images.

3. Convert fewer, better pictures

Remove duplicates and blurry shots. Every extra page adds to the size.

4. Pick the right page size

A4 or Letter pages scale the image to fit, but the embedded picture data is the same. Quality settings have the biggest impact on size.

Ready to try? Open the image to PDF converter and set Quality to Medium.
