JPG vs PNG vs WebP: Which Image Format Should You Use?

Updated: October 30, 2026

All three formats store pictures, but each is best at something different.

JPG / JPEG

Best for photographs. It uses lossy compression to make small files. Not ideal for sharp text or logos, and it does not support transparency.

PNG

Best for screenshots, logos and graphics with text. It is lossless and supports transparent backgrounds, but photo files are larger than JPG.

WebP

A newer format from Google that is usually smaller than both JPG and PNG at similar quality and supports transparency. Support in older software is still patchy.

Which should you pick for PDF?

For documents and screenshots use PNG; for photos use JPG. If your files are in WebP and need to be shared or printed, convert them with our WebP to PDF tool, which works on any device.
